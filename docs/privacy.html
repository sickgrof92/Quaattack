Quaattack! Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Quaattack! is a game for phones. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The short version: your game stays on your device, and the only information that leaves it is a record of any purchases you make.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, scores, power-ups, daily streak and chosen styles are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Purchases are optional and are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

To deliver purchases and restore them on a new device, the game uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives a random ID created for your installation, your device type and operating system, your country and currency, and your purchase history from the store. This information is used to deliver what you buy, restore purchases, and count sales. It is encrypted in transit, and it is not sold or used for advertising.

What we don't do

The game has no ads, no accounts, no chat and no tracking across other apps or websites. It doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Children

Quaattack! is made for a general audience. Every purchase first asks a written-out math question meant for grown-ups and older kids. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your purchase records from RevenueCat by contacting us below. Store receipts are kept by Google or Apple under their own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
